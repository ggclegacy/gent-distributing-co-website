## Performance and fidelity limits

Rendering is demand-driven; a stationary film does not maintain an idle render loop. High and standard geometry tiers cap DPR at 1.5 and 1.25 respectively, with no postprocessing or external asset/font services. GSAP clock lag smoothing is disabled for the scroll film so long GPU frames do not stretch the scrub recovery indefinitely.

These checks use desktop browser engines and phone-sized viewports, not a physical iPhone battery/thermal/FPS certification. The largest individual generated JavaScript chunk is approximately 539KB raw / 140KB gzip; the 3D renderer is lazy-loaded. Network transfer depends on the eventual hosting compression configuration.

The Exchange and products are deliberately modular procedural models. They are production-integrated geometry, not final commissioned photorealistic Blender assets. Final packaging and material scans can replace them through the documented asset contract. No public deployment was performed.
