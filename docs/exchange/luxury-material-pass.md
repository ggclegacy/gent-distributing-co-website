# Exchange material and geometry refinement

The existing nine-beat film and its named animation pivots are retained. This pass replaces tubular outlines with beveled machined bands, inset category faces, structural lugs, fasteners, narrow gold inlays, and a continuous extruded Gent mark.

Product concepts now use a shaped and gusseted coffee pouch with conforming foil print, lathed dark bottles with defined shoulders, knurled closures and curved labels, folded cloth, and a stitched leather case. The palette is black, deep green and restrained gold. These remain procedural product concepts, not manufacturing-ready packaging or commissioned photorealistic assets.

A local photographic reflection stage supplies shaped softbox reflections without external downloads. Neutral tone mapping preserves brand colors; low-resolution shadows and reduced geometry/DPR remain available on smaller screens. The existing optional GLB replacement contract is unchanged.

Campaign fallback images are exported from the actual final canvas for desktop and portrait. Existing reduced-motion, motion toggle, navigation, master scroll playhead, and portal handoff are preserved.
