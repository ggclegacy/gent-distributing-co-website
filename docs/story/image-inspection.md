# Asset inspection

Built-in image_gen produced exactly eight images, one call per image. All eight were visually inspected. Landscape images are 1536 x 1024; portrait images are 1024 x 1536 and use independently generated vertical compositions.

All scenes use realistic products, hands, people, grounded packages and architecture. Both product-vault images show the requested Legacy Reserve / Signature Blend coffee text with the other product categories clearly represented. Mobile upper architecture leaves useful title space; desktop upper left areas support overlays.

Minor generation limitations: some distant packaging contains tiny synthetic label marks. Ten foreground product categories are present in the vault but its background does not literally show ten discrete occupied bays. The portrait vault coffee pouch remains visually central and taller, though the other categories are clearly visible. These are illustration concepts and packaging should not be treated as production label artwork.
